Three directions

All three read the same live endpoints and the same normalised model, so what you are comparing is the design, not the data. None of them invents a figure: ProWood, BayCo and B3 have no endpoint on this server and every direction has to say so in its own way.

Direction A

Standup

One column, read top to bottom, done in thirty seconds. No cards — rules and space do the grouping. Colour only marks what is yours to answer.

Best if the daily question is “what do I need to know?”
 Direction B

Lanes

Ventures as rows, columns always in the same place, so you scan down a column instead of reading a page. Densest of the three.

Best if the daily question is “where does each thing stand?”
 Direction C

Cockpit

Two panes: what needs deciding on the left, the run you are working on the right, with a follow-up box under it. Built around steering rather than watching.

Best if the daily question is “what am I working on right now?”
These are throwaway prototypes in try/. Nothing here replaces a page you use; the live Command Center is untouched. Pick one — or parts of several — and that becomes the build.
